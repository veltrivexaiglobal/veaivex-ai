## 📌 Executive Overview
**VEAIVEX AI** transforms raw POS, expense, inventory, and customer ledgers into instant root-cause variance explanations (*"Why did net profit fall?"*), predictive stockout alerts, and prioritized ROI operational actions in clear Executive English.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Modules

1. **Executive Scorecard & Business Health Index (0-100)**: Weighted 6-pillar financial and operational health scoring.
2. **Deterministic Root-Cause "Why?" Engine**: Mathematical ledger reconciliation isolating exact cost spikes and gross margin variances.
3. **Interactive POS & Counter Sales**: Visual catalog, real-time inventory deduction, margin tracking, and digital receipts.
4. **Predictive Inventory & Lead-Time Burn**: Forecasting stockout horizons against supplier turnaround cycles.
5. **Customer Intelligence & RFM Churn Radar**: Automated VIP account tracking and retention alerts.
6. **What-If Scenario Sandbox**: Real-time modeling of price elasticity, generator fuel overhead cuts, and solar investments.
7. **Conversational Voice BI Copilot**: Hands-free natural speech and text analytics with verified data citations.
